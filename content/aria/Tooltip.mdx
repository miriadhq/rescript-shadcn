## Usage

```res showLineNumbers
<Tooltip.Trigger>
  <Button variant=Outline> {"Hover"->React.string} </Button>
  <Tooltip>
    <p> {"Add to library"->React.string} </p>
  </Tooltip>
</Tooltip.Trigger>
```
