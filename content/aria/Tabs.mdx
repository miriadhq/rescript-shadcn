## Usage

```res showLineNumbers
<Tabs defaultSelectedKey="account" className="w-[400px]">
  <Tabs.List>
    <Tabs.Trigger id="account"> {"Account"->React.string} </Tabs.Trigger>
    <Tabs.Trigger id="password"> {"Password"->React.string} </Tabs.Trigger>
  </Tabs.List>
  <Tabs.Content id="account"> {"Make changes to your account here."->React.string} </Tabs.Content>
  <Tabs.Content id="password"> {"Change your password here."->React.string} </Tabs.Content>
</Tabs>
```
